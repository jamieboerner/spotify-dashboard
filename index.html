Chorus — The Conversion Gap
Why is 45% of the platform free?
The platform works. The business model doesn't. 22,525 fully engaged users generating zero subscription revenue is not a product problem — it's a conversion problem.

Bridge — Feature Demand
No single feature wins. Every feature is wanted.
Free users want all 6 desired features almost equally. The spread between most and least desired is just 71 users out of 22,525. There is no single conversion lever — personalised nudges are needed.
Desired Features — Free Users
User count per desired feature (Free tier only)
71 users separate #1 (Better AI Recommendations, 3,780) from #6 (Lyrics Translation, 3,709). Demand is perfectly flat — no feature dominates.
Desired Features — All Tiers
Overall feature demand across all 50,000 users
Across all tiers, the same flat distribution holds. Mood-based Auto Playlists leads overall (8,419) but the spread is only 188 users from #1 to #6.

Action 1
Target the engaged
Deploy personalised Premium trials to Free users above 8 hours/week listening time. No new marketing spend required — the users already exist.
Action 2
Personalise the pitch
Show each Free user the feature they ranked #1. Not a one-size-fits-all campaign — 22,525 users want 22,525 different things.
Action 3
Price for the market
India is the largest market. USA is the smallest. Both on identical pricing. Introduce regional pricing tiers — lower entry price, higher conversion volume.
